# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

BookNest is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for independent libraries and study spaces.

The platform allows library owners to manage their businesses through a web-based dashboard while students and visitors can discover libraries through a public website.

BookNest itself is the SaaS provider. Libraries are its paying customers. Students are customers of individual libraries, not BookNest directly in V1.

### Main objectives

- Digitize library operations.
- Manage library staff and permissions.
- Manage students and memberships.
- Configure study plans and time slots.
- Track attendance.
- Manage student payments, deposits and outstanding balances.
- Provide financial reports.
- Allow students to discover libraries and contact them.
- Provide a scalable SaaS foundation for future features.

# 2. BUSINESS MODEL

There are two separate financial relationships.

### A. BookNest subscription

Library owners subscribe to BookNest.

Planned subscription options:
- 30-day free trial
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Yearly
- Five-year

### B. Library student finances

Libraries independently manage:
- Membership fees
- Advance deposits
- Payments
- Discounts
- Additional charges
- Credits and adjustments
- Refunds
- Outstanding dues

These two financial domains must remain separate in the application and database design.

# 3. USER ROLES

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Platform Admin | Manage the BookNest SaaS platform |
| Library Owner | Manage their library and business |
| Librarian/Staff | Perform authorized library operations |
| Student/Public Visitor | Discover libraries and contact them |

Students will not have login accounts in V1.

Only library owners and authorized staff can create student records and memberships.

- View library profiles.
- View facilities and amenities.
- View operating hours.
- View study plans and available options.
- View public library information.
- Click to reveal the library/librarian contact number.
- Contact the library directly.

No in-platform appointment scheduling or student self-service booking in V1.

## Library management dashboard

- Update member details.

### Membership management
- Create memberships.
- Support monthly, quarterly, yearly and custom durations.
- Support different plans.
- Support individual agreed pricing.
- Support discounts and adjustments.
- Preserve original membership terms when standard pricing changes.

### Financial management
- Record payments.
- Record advance deposits.
- Track outstanding balances.
- Apply discounts.
- Record additional charges.
- Record credits and adjustments.
- Handle refunds.
- Maintain an auditable transaction history.

### Attendance
- Record and manage student attendance.

### Reports
- Membership reports.
- Student reports.
- Attendance reports.

# 6. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES

1. Each library is an independent tenant.
2. Every tenant-owned record must be associated with its tenant/library.
3. Backend authorization must determine tenant access from authenticated identity.
4. Frontend-provided tenant IDs must never be trusted as authorization.
5. Cross-tenant data access must be prevented and tested.
6. A library can configure its own operating hours.
7. Each library can create custom study plans and access slots.
8. Membership duration and daily study-plan duration are separate concepts.
9. Each plan can have different standard prices.
10. Individual students can have negotiated prices.
11. Existing memberships retain their original agreed terms.
12. Financial balances must be derived from transactions, not manually overwritten.
13. Financial corrections must be recorded through adjustment or reversal transactions.
14. BookNest subscription billing and library student finances must remain separate.

# 7. STUDY PLAN DESIGN

Study plans must be configurable rather than hardcoded.

Example:

Library operating hours: 6 AM–10 PM.

Possible plans:
- Morning: 8 AM–2 PM.
- Evening: 2 PM–8 PM.
- Full Day: 8 hours of permitted access.

Different libraries may offer:
- One plan covering their permitted operating hours.
- Two daily shifts.
- Three daily shifts.
- Custom access arrangements.

Pricing is configurable by the library owner.

Membership duration is independent of the daily access plan.

Example:
A student may purchase a monthly membership for the Morning plan.

# 8. FINANCIAL DESIGN

Use an auditable ledger.

Example:

- Advance deposit: +₹2,000
- Membership charge: -₹1,000
- Additional charge: -₹100
- Remaining balance: ₹900

Every financial operation must be traceable.

Store relevant information such as:
- Amount
- Transaction type
- Date
- Payment method
- Reference
- Related member/membership
- Recorded by
- Reason or description

Initial offline payment methods may include cash, UPI and bank transfer.

Online payment gateway selection is pending.

# 12. SECURITY AND PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

- Secure authentication.
- Role-based access control.
- Tenant isolation.
- Server-side authorization.
- Input validation.
- Secure password hashing.
- HTTPS in production.
- Environment-based secrets.
- Rate limiting.
- Audit logging.
- Database backups.
- Error handling and monitoring.
- Automated tests.
- Secure handling of sensitive personal information.

Avoid collecting full Aadhaar numbers by default. Any decision to collect them requires a documented necessity and privacy/legal review, with encryption, restricted access and retention controls.

# 13. DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

The agreed engineering process is:

1. SRS — Software Requirements Specification
2. HLD — High-Level Design
3. LLD — Low-Level Design
4. UI/UX Design
5. Implementation
6. Testing
7. Deployment

We will not rush into building all modules at once.

We will develop one module at a time, verify it, test it and then proceed.
